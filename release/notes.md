# Cybex OS 1.0.12

- The menubar's music player is redesigned. The chip keeps one width: the cover, the track, and a play/pause button whose ring shows how far the track has played. Its controls no longer slide out under the pointer and push the rest of the bar aside. Paused is visible at rest: the title dims and the cover greys. A middle click skips to the next track, and the scroll wheel sets that player's own volume, with the volume display naming the player. When a second player has something to show, its cover sits behind the first.
- The music panel has a centred transport (shuffle, previous, play, next, repeat), with shuffle and repeat hidden for players that lack them. Its seek bar shows the time under the pointer, and a click on the end time shows the time remaining. It also adds a volume slider for the player itself, a button that brings the player's window forward, and a list of the other players to switch to or play and pause in place. Live streams say so. Keys: Space plays or pauses, the arrows seek and change the volume, and Shift with an arrow skips.
- A player picked in the panel stays in the menubar until another player starts playing. A second player started from the list no longer swaps places with the one shown.

Validation covers source regressions (`tests/run`, QML lint), a new real-shell test that drives the menubar chip and panel under headless Sway with fake MPRIS players and real pointer and keyboard input, in the dark and light themes, Nix evaluation, the shared installation-contract parity check and the actual local staged-updater lifecycle in an isolated NixOS VM. The test has no PipeWire, so it exercises the MPRIS volume fallback. The PipeWire per-player volume path is covered by unit tests only.

This is a source release with no new ISO attached. The existing 1.0.10 testing ISO retains its original source and does not contain these changes. Fresh ISO installation, applying the release through a published channel, a real Hyprland session with real players, and physical hardware qualification have not been run for this release revision.
